The paper is a careful and unusually candid report: its arithmetic is largely clean (Tables 1-5, t-intervals and Wilson interval recompute), and it states most of its own limits. The main problems are claims that still run ahead of the evidence. The abstract says the failed gate "points to a defect in the pipeline" although Section 4.6 admits this was not tested; the "null result" label sits uneasily with detected declines and a gate that fails under any training signal; and the power statements (2.2 vs 3.5-5.5 points, the 2.8h/1.96 rule) are not reconciled. The "independent" grader and the r_t audit both depend on the same \boxed-share drift they are meant to check. One numeric error in the hash-bias control (difference -0.1 vs the stated falls of 4.9 and 3.1) was verified from the saved generations and corrected to -1.8 [-7.4, 3.8]. These issues are fixable in text; the open gaps need new experiments (a valid positive control, independent grading of earlier runs).
