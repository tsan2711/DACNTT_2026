The paper is honest about its limits and its arithmetic largely checks out (t-intervals, detectable-effect formula, Table 1 vs Table 3, the r_t identity). The core problem is that the positive gate fails and appears confounded with the \boxed dependence of both graders, so the 'null for the recipe' is inconclusive and the power statements are optimistic. The final round also shows a consistent decline that matches the boxed-share drop, which sits awkwardly under the 'null' label, and seven simultaneous recipe changes prevent attribution. Remaining issues are mostly framing and small internal inconsistencies, which are fixable in the text; a valid positive control would need new experiments.
